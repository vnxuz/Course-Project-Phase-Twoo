Our Story

Founded in 1904 by Alfred Amour Garnier, our brand was built on a simple belief: beauty should be accessible to everyone. From our first hair lotion to today's innovative plant-based formulas, we've never stopped evolving.

In 2020, we launched the Garnier Green Beauty initiative, committing to more sustainable packaging, responsible sourcing, and transparent formulas. Today, over 95% of our hair care bottles are made from 100% recycled plastic.

We're proud to be a Leaping Bunny certified brand, ensuring none of our products are tested on animals.

🌍 Our Green Beauty Promise

By 2025, 100% of our plastic packaging will be reusable, recyclable, or compostable. We're on a mission to make beauty better for everyone.
